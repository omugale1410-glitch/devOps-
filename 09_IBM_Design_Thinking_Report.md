# 9. IBM Design Thinking Report

## 9.1 Focus on User Outcomes

The product should be evaluated by the work users can complete successfully. Examples include locating a student record, submitting attendance or obtaining an academic report. Screen count or feature count alone is not a useful measure of product value.

## 9.2 Restless Reinvention

The design should remain open to revision. Evidence from interviews, prototype tests and actual workflow observations should be allowed to change requirements and interface decisions.

## 9.3 Diverse, Empowered Teams

Students, faculty, administrators, examination personnel and technical staff bring different perspectives. Their participation reduces the risk of designing the system around only one workflow.

## 9.4 Observe → Reflect → Make

The team should repeatedly:

1. **Observe** user behavior and problems.
2. **Reflect** on what the evidence means.
3. **Make** a change or prototype.
4. Return to observation and evaluate the result.

## 9.5 Application to This Project

For example, if attendance entry requires too many actions during a class, observation identifies the issue, reflection determines the cause, and the next prototype can simplify the workflow. The revised workflow can then be tested again.
