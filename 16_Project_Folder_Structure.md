# 16. Project Folder Structure

A possible repository organization is:

```text
student-academic-platform/
│
├── README.md
├── docs/
│   ├── BRD.md
│   ├── Product-Vision.md
│   ├── Stakeholder-Analysis.md
│   ├── User-Personas.md
│   ├── FRS.md
│   ├── NFRS.md
│   ├── Design-Thinking.md
│   ├── IBM-Design-Thinking.md
│   ├── IBM-Loop.md
│   ├── MVP.md
│   ├── Product-Roadmap.md
│   ├── Architecture.md
│   ├── Project-Charter.md
│   └── Product-Discovery.md
│
├── prototype/
│   └── wireframes/
│
├── src/
│   ├── frontend/
│   ├── backend/
│   └── database/
│
├── tests/
└── assets/
```

This structure separates product documentation, prototypes, implementation, testing and supporting assets. It can be adjusted once the actual technology stack is selected.
