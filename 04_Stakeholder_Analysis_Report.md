# 4. Stakeholder Analysis Report

Stakeholder analysis identifies who interacts with the proposed product, what each group needs, and where their input is required during product development.

## 4.1 Stakeholder Map

| Stakeholder | Responsibility | Main Need | Typical Difficulty | Project Contribution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Student | Uses academic services | Current profile, attendance and results | Information may be distributed across channels | Validates student workflows |
| Faculty | Conducts academic activities | Quick attendance and student-record access | Repetitive data entry | Defines and tests teaching workflows |
| Academic Administrator | Maintains institutional records | Centralized and controlled records | Managing large volumes of information | Validates administration requirements |
| Examination Staff | Handles examination data | Accurate entry, verification and publication | Maintaining result consistency | Defines examination workflow |
| IT Support | Maintains technology | Secure and reliable operation | Troubleshooting and maintenance | Advises on technical constraints |

## 4.2 Engagement Approach

Students and faculty should be involved early because they perform the most frequent day-to-day tasks. Administrative and examination users should validate permissions, reporting and record-control requirements. IT support should review security, deployment and maintenance considerations.

## 4.3 Stakeholder Risk

A design created without direct user feedback may reproduce existing process problems in digital form. Therefore, requirements should be reviewed and tested with representatives of the relevant stakeholder groups.
